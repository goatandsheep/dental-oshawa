Our Mission

Exceptional care,
every visit.

At Dental Oshawa, we take pride in our legacy of providing exceptional dental care to our valued patients for over 27 years. As a dedicated clinic, we focus on the diagnosis and treatment of different problems related to patients' teeth, gums, and other parts of the mouth.

We believe in a holistic approach to dental health, which helps the prevention of dental disorders. Our specialists offer instruction and advice on how to take care of your teeth and gums, including the recommended dietary choices that may impact your overall oral health.

Whether you're seeking dental implants, periodontal treatments, or endodontic care, our team of skilled professionals is here to serve you.

Dental Implants

Periodontics

We offer comprehensive periodontal care, from scaling and root planing to gum grafts and pocket reduction surgery. Regular check-ups are essential for maintaining optimal gum health.

Learn more

Endodontics (Root Canal Therapy)

When tooth nerves become injured or infected, our skilled endodontists step in to save your natural teeth. Root canal therapy (RCT) removes infected pulp, relieves pain, and restores tooth function.

The Team

Dentist · Endodontist · Periodontist

Dr. Mandeep Dhillon

Dentist, Endodontist, Periodontist

Mandeep has been practicing dentistry since 1984. He studied dentistry at University of Toronto and later obtained a certification in periodontics from the University of Dalhousie.

In the pursuit of knowledge, he went on to be part of the first to obtain an endodontic certification in Canada from the University of Toronto.

Mandeep loves travelling, spending time on his boat, and watching the stars at night with his telescope.
